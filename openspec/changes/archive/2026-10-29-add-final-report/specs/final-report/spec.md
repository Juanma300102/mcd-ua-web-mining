## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The report answers the questions of docs/def.md in narrative order
`docs/informe.html` SHALL be a Spanish HTML page with one chapter per question of `docs/def.md`, in this order: change in tone over time, differences between the digital, traditional and provincial outlets, milestones, and provincial tone. Each chapter heading SHALL state the chapter's answer as a sentence, not a topic. Each chapter SHALL have one main chart and a short plain-language paragraph saying what the chart means. The page SHALL open with the main question and close with the three answers, one sentence each.

#### Scenario: Chapter headings state the answer
- **GIVEN** the built report
- **WHEN** a reader skims only the chapter headings
- **THEN** they read one answer per question of `docs/def.md`, for example "El tono casi no se movió en tres años" rather than "Evolución temporal"

#### Scenario: Closing answers
- **GIVEN** the built report
- **WHEN** a reader reaches the closing section
- **THEN** it gives the answer to the main question and to both secondary questions, one sentence each

### Requirement: Technical detail lives in a collapsible annex
The narrative chapters SHALL NOT use statistical or engineering terms (F1, Welch, t interval, partition, asset, Parquet) without a plain-language gloss. The page SHALL end with a technical annex inside a `<details>` element, closed by default, covering the data pipeline, the robots.txt constraint, the model evaluation (macro F1 and confusion per model, from `docs/decision-sentimiento.md`), how the intervals and windows are computed, and the command that rebuilds the report.

#### Scenario: Annex closed by default
- **GIVEN** the report opened in a browser
- **WHEN** the page loads
- **THEN** the technical annex is collapsed and the narrative reads end to end without opening it

#### Scenario: Model evaluation in the annex
- **GIVEN** the annex is opened
- **WHEN** a technical reader looks for the model choice
- **THEN** it shows the accuracy and macro F1 of robertuito, mdeberta-xnli and qwen2.5-7b and says the labels were generated by Claude Opus 5.5 without human review

### Requirement: Every number comes from an embedded data block built from the stored Parquet files
A build script SHALL read `data/sentimiento_noticias.parquet` and `data/noticias_politica.parquet`, compute every number and series the page shows, and write them as a single JSON block (`<script type="application/json" id="datos">`) inside `docs/informe.html`, replacing the previous block and leaving the rest of the file unchanged. The page SHALL draw every chart and fill every number from that block. The narrative SHALL use the final model (`MODELO_FINAL`) only; the other models SHALL appear only where the page compares models.

#### Scenario: Rebuild replaces only the data block
- **GIVEN** `docs/informe.html` with an existing data block and the two Parquet files in `data/`
- **WHEN** `uv run python scripts/build_report.py` runs
- **THEN** only the content of the `datos` block changes, and running it a second time leaves the file byte-identical

#### Scenario: Missing data
- **GIVEN** `data/sentimiento_noticias.parquet` does not exist
- **WHEN** the build script runs
- **THEN** it exits with a non-zero status and a message naming the missing file, and `docs/informe.html` is unchanged

#### Scenario: Page and data agree on keys
- **GIVEN** the keys the page reads from the data block
- **WHEN** the test suite runs
- **THEN** every key the page reads exists in the JSON that the aggregation produces

### Requirement: Tone over time is shown monthly per outlet
The data block SHALL hold, per outlet and calendar month in Argentina time, the number of notes, the mean score of the final model and its 95 % interval, with the same exclusions as `evolucion_tono` (live notes, unscored notes, notes without a date). Months with fewer than two notes SHALL carry no interval. The milestones of `anotaciones/hitos.csv` with a date SHALL be marked on the chart.

#### Scenario: Monthly point
- **GIVEN** an outlet with scores 0.2, 0.4, 0.0 and -0.2 in March 2025 and one live note that month
- **WHEN** the monthly series is computed
- **THEN** March 2025 has `n` 4 and mean 0.1, and the live note is not counted

#### Scenario: Month with one note
- **GIVEN** an outlet with a single scored note in a month
- **WHEN** the monthly series is computed
- **THEN** that month has a mean and no interval

### Requirement: Outlets are compared on mean tone and label shares
The data block SHALL hold, per outlet, the number of notes, the mean score and the share of negative, neutral and positive labels of the final model, without live notes. It SHALL also hold the mean score per outlet for every model in `sentimiento_noticias`, so the page can show that the ordering of the outlets does not depend on the model.

#### Scenario: Shares sum to one
- **GIVEN** an outlet with 2 negative, 1 neutral and 1 positive note
- **WHEN** the outlet comparison is computed
- **THEN** its shares are 0.5, 0.25 and 0.25

### Requirement: Milestone changes are shown with their uncertainty
The milestone chapter SHALL use `compare_milestones` from `news_politica.sentiment.tone` unchanged, for the final model. A change SHALL be highlighted only when its interval excludes zero; every other change SHALL be drawn as "not distinguishable", and windows marked `insuficiente` SHALL show no before/after mean. Any highlighted change SHALL state its number of notes before and after in the text.

#### Scenario: Interval includes zero
- **GIVEN** a milestone and outlet whose difference interval is (-0.33, 0.27)
- **WHEN** the milestone chart is drawn
- **THEN** that change is drawn in the neutral style and not described as a change

#### Scenario: Insufficient window
- **GIVEN** a milestone whose "before" window has fewer than `N_MIN` notes for an outlet
- **WHEN** the milestone chart is drawn
- **THEN** that outlet shows no before mean and the window is labelled as having too few notes

### Requirement: Provincial tone is shown with and without reproduced national notes
The data block SHALL split MisionesOnline notes into its own notes (empty `fuente_externa`) and notes reproduced from other outlets (non-empty `fuente_externa`), with `n` and mean score per group, for every model. The page SHALL show both groups next to the national outlets, as `docs/def.md` asks for the provincial comparison with and without agency notes.

#### Scenario: Split by fuente_externa
- **GIVEN** MisionesOnline notes where 3 have an empty `fuente_externa` and 2 have "TN"
- **WHEN** the provincial split is computed
- **THEN** the own group has `n` 3 and the reproduced group has `n` 2

### Requirement: The report states its limitations in the narrative
The page SHALL say, before the first finding, that tone is not stance toward the government. A limitations section before the annex SHALL state: Infobae and La Nación are a sample of 30 notes per month while MisionesOnline is complete; robertuito was trained on tweets; MisionesOnline's bajada coverage changes over time; and La Nación's robots.txt forbids its frontend API, so only sitemaps and note HTML were used.

#### Scenario: Stance caveat comes first
- **GIVEN** the built report
- **WHEN** a reader scrolls from the top
- **THEN** the sentence that tone is not stance appears before the first chapter with a finding

### Requirement: The report explains the technologies used in each step
The page SHALL have a section, before the limitations, that walks through the process step by step (find the notes, download them, extract and merge the text, score the tone, summarize it, build the report) and, for each step, names the technologies that act in it and what each one does there in plain language: how each outlet was scraped (sitemaps, the WordPress API, the embedded Arc XP JSON, robots.txt, the request interval and retries), the pipeline (Dagster, Postgres in Docker, Parquet, pandas, uv, pytest) and the machine learning (the three models, PyTorch, Transformers, pysentimiento, Ollama, SciPy). It SHALL include a table with one row per Dagster asset saying what the asset does, what it reads and how it runs. The silver-label caveat SHALL be stated in the technical annex, not in the limitations list.

#### Scenario: Every asset is explained
- **GIVEN** the built report
- **WHEN** a reader opens the technology section
- **THEN** it has one row for each of `catalogo_infobae`, `catalogo_lanacion`, `raw_infobae`, `raw_lanacion`, `raw_misionesonline`, `noticias_politica`, `sentimiento_noticias` and `evolucion_tono`

#### Scenario: Model accuracy comes from the data block
- **GIVEN** the technology section's model table
- **WHEN** the page loads
- **THEN** the accuracy of each model is filled from the `evaluacion` entries of the data block

### Requirement: The data pipeline is shown with annotated Dagster screenshots
The page SHALL include two screenshots of the Dagster UI, committed under `docs/img/`: the global asset lineage and the partitions view of a `raw_*` asset with its 34 monthly partitions materialized. Each SHALL carry a plain-language caption. The lineage caption SHALL say that the sentiment assets ran on another machine (with a GPU) and therefore show as not materialized in this UI.

#### Scenario: Images present
- **GIVEN** the built report
- **WHEN** it is opened from `docs/`
- **THEN** both screenshots load from `docs/img/` and each has a caption and alt text

### Requirement: The page follows the visual system of the phase log and works on any screen
The page SHALL reuse the fonts, colour tokens and per-outlet colours of `docs/bitacora-fases.html`, SHALL support light and dark mode, SHALL draw its charts as inline SVG without charting libraries, SHALL load nothing but Google Fonts from the network, and SHALL have no horizontal scroll at a 375 px viewport.

#### Scenario: Phone width
- **GIVEN** the report at a 375 px wide viewport
- **WHEN** the page is scrolled top to bottom
- **THEN** no element causes horizontal scrolling and every chart stays readable

### Requirement: Report aggregations are tested offline
The aggregation functions SHALL be pure (pandas and the standard library, no Dagster, no models, no network) and SHALL be covered by tests that build small synthetic frames, so `uv run pytest` needs neither the Parquet files nor the `sentiment` group.

#### Scenario: Tests without data
- **GIVEN** a checkout with an empty `data/` directory and without the `sentiment` group
- **WHEN** `uv run pytest` runs
- **THEN** the report tests pass
